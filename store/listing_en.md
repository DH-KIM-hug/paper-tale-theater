## 설명 본문 (그대로 붙여 넣기)

```
Tok Tok Tale Theater is a storybook app for ages 2 to 5. Children step into fairy tales cut from paper and help tell the story.

DAYTIME: TAP AND PLAY
Children tap, choose and put things in order to keep the story going. A wrong answer is never punished. Taps appear only where the story has a reason for them.
- Folk tales, Aesop fables and classics such as The Tortoise and the Hare, The Enormous Turnip, Cinderella and the Tiger and the Red Bean Porridge
- Gentle activities in each tale: counting, comparing sizes, ordering, sorting, spotting differences, listening for sounds and thinking about feelings
- Every line is read aloud in a warm voice, with the words shown on screen

NIGHTTIME: BEDTIME STORIES
Choose Read Aloud and the app reads a tale from start to finish. It keeps playing with the screen off and moves on to the next tale. Tales are read in Korean, and in English for the tales that have an English reading.

STICKER BOOK AND MY STAGE
Finish a tale by playing it and collect a sticker. Use your stickers to decorate your own little stage.

HOW IT WORKS
- The app is free to download. Four tales are always free, original tales are free, and four more tales are free each week.
- Finish a tale to earn a star piece. Five star pieces open one locked tale.
- Unlock All (one-time purchase, $4.99) opens every tale and lets a parent change the main character's name in the original tales to your child's name. One name change is included. Changing the name again needs a Name Change Pass ($0.99).
- No ads. No sign-up or login.
- Tales work offline. The internet is used only to link devices and confirm purchases.

FOR PARENTS
- Purchases, device linking and outside documents are behind a grown-up check (hold the right answer to a multiplication question for 1.5 seconds).
- In the grown-up menu (hold the gear at the top right for 1 second) you can set sound, play time, language, captions and auto play.
- Privacy policy: https://dh-kim-hug.github.io/paper-tale-theater/privacy.html
- Contact: hello@ddoystory.com

Artwork was made with OpenAI image generation tools. Voices were recorded with ElevenLabs.
```
